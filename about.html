About

I’m a designer who writes code, so I can prototype an idea in an afternoon and tell you honestly whether it works.

I grew up between Lagos and Lisbon and started out building websites for local shops. That taught me to care about the person using the thing, not just how it looks.

Since then I’ve spent seven years on product teams, designing tools for healthcare, finance and research. In 2026 I went independent to work with fewer teams, more closely.
