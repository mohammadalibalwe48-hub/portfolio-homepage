Work

Six projects from the last five years. Tap a project to see what I did and what changed.

  • A note-taking app for field researchers that works fully offline. I designed a sync model people could actually understand, then built the interface in React.

  • Rebuilt checkout for a payments startup. Fewer fields, clearer errors, and one screen instead of four.

    Checkout completion rose from 61% to 78%.

    Design-to-code handoff dropped from five days to one.

  • A tiny app that sends one hand-lettered postcard to one friend every week. I made it to stay in touch and kept it going.

    Still running, 190 postcards and counting.
