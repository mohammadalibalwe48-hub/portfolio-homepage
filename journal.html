Short notes on design, code and the small decisions that make products feel right.

Why I stopped designing in grey boxesOct 2026, 3 min

Grey wireframes hide the hard questions. Real words, real data and real error states show you what breaks.

Now I start with actual content, even if it’s rough. The layout follows from it, and the team argues about the right things sooner.

A checkout form is a conversationAug 2026, 4 min

Every field is a question. If you wouldn’t ask it out loud to someone at a shop counter, ask whether you need it at all.

When we cut four fields at Greenhouse Pay, completion went up. When we rewrote the error messages, it went up again.

Color blindness is not an edge caseMay 2026, 5 min

About one in twelve men has some form of color vision deficiency. On a transit map, that is a lot of riders.

Pair color with shape or a label, and test your palette in a simulator before you ship it.

What a design system is really forFeb 2026, 4 min

It isn’t a library of buttons. It’s an agreement about how decisions get made, written down so nobody has to make them twice.

Start with the five components used on every screen, and document why they look the way they do.
